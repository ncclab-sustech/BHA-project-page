Here is the idea underneath BHA. Brains and vision models are looking at the same physical world, and the better they get, the more their representations converge toward it: the platonic representation hypothesis. We take it one step further. There is one world, objective and invariant. And there are many ways of seeing it: CLIP's, DINO's, MAE's, and your brain's, each partial and subjective. So a decoder should not imitate one view. It should learn from all of them, because together they describe the world the brain is also looking at.
